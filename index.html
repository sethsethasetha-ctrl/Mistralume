ORBILUME

Plan Every Orbit

A rhythm puzzle in orbit

Guide cargo pods through a luminous network of circular tracks. Build a 12-beat plan, activate transfer gates at the right moment, and deliver every pod before the city pressure fills.

Plan the route

Choose an IN or OUT gate, select a signal, and place it on the beat timeline. Your plan becomes the route every vehicle follows.

Learn new signals

Use SHIFT for direct activation, HOLD for delayed timing, LINK for paired gates, and ECHO to repeat a signal in the next cycle.

Gameplay

  • 20 orbital planning puzzles
  • A clear 12-beat rhythm timeline
  • Multiple tracks, directional gates, and matching stations
  • Saved progress, pause, retry, and level select
  • Optional atmospheric background music
  • Offline play with no account required
