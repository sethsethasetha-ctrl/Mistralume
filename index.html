Discover the flow

Every transfer is a decision.

Vehicles circle glowing tracks automatically. Tap a transfer gate to reserve it for the next eligible vehicle, then watch your plan unfold. A reservation is consumed by the next arrival, so timing and route awareness are everything.

Reserve

Choose a transfer gate and reserve the next vehicle that reaches it. Cancel before it activates if your plan changes.

Deliver

Guide each color-and-symbol matched vehicle to its correct station through connected orbital tracks.

Protect the city

Avoid collisions and missed deliveries. Keep the five-step City Pressure meter below its limit.
