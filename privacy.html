Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zichen Play Studio (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in Orbilume (the “App”).

Information the App stores on your device

Orbilume stores your level progress, earned stars, and background-music preference locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the App’s gameplay and settings. We do not operate an account system or a server for this local progress data.

Advertising and App Tracking Transparency

The App may display advertisements between certain levels. Where an advertising provider requests permission to track activity across apps or websites, iOS will ask for your permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before such tracking is enabled. You may decline permission; declining does not limit core gameplay, saved progress, or music controls.

Advertising providers may process information as described in their own privacy policies and as disclosed in the App Store privacy information for the released version of the App. We do not sell your personal information.

Information we do not ask you to provide

The App does not ask you to create an account or submit your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone input, or payment information.

Children’s privacy

Orbilume is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been provided to us through the App, please contact the developer using the contact method on the App Store product page so we can investigate the request.

Data security and retention

Local gameplay preferences remain on your device until you remove the App or clear its data through your device settings. Because we do not receive your local progress data, we cannot retrieve or restore it for you.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App, its advertising providers, or applicable legal requirements change. We will publish the updated policy at this page and revise the “Last updated” date.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact method listed on the App Store product page for Orbilume.
