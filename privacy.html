Orbilume Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

Overview

Orbilume is an offline puzzle game. We respect your privacy and designed the app to work without an account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or tracking technology.

Information We Collect

Orbilume does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information. The app does not request tracking permission and does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or security services.

On-Device Data

The app stores your highest unlocked level and background-music preference locally on your device so that the game can remember your settings. This information is not sent off your device and is removed when you delete the app or clear its data.

Children’s Privacy

Because Orbilume does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the developer through the Orbilume product page on the App Store.
