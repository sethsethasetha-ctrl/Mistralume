Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zichen Play Studio (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in Mistralume (the “App”).

Information We Handle

Mistralume does not require you to create an account. Gameplay progress, preferences, and local settings are stored on your device using the operating system’s local storage.

Advertising and App Tracking Transparency

The App may display advertising through third-party advertising providers. Before any provider uses data for tracking across apps or websites, the App requests permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework where required by Apple policy. You can allow or deny tracking in the system prompt and later change your choice in your device settings.

Advertising providers may process device, diagnostic, and advertising-related information in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not sell personal information ourselves.

How Information Is Used

  • To save game progress and audio preferences on your device.
  • To deliver and measure advertising where applicable and permitted.
  • To maintain the security, stability, and functionality of the App.

Data Sharing

We do not share your local game progress with third parties. If advertising services are active, limited information may be shared with those providers only to provide their services and as described in their privacy policies.

Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us through the developer support contact listed on the App Store product page.

Security

We use reasonable measures designed to protect the App and its local data. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App or applicable requirements change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when changes are posted.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer support contact shown on the Mistralume App Store product page.
